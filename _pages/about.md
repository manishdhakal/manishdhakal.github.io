<b>CV/NLP</b>

Manish Dhakal is a Ph.D. student in Industrial Engineering at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, supervised by Dr. Yi Ding. He began his doctoral studies in Computer Science at Georgia State University in 2024 and transferred to UTK in 2026 when his advisor's lab moved there, earning an M.S. in Computer Science en route. His primary line of research is within <b>model compression</b> and <b>efficient learning</b> of computer vision (CV) and natural language processing (NLP) applications. His publications have been presented at premier venues, such as EMNLP, WACV, MICCAI, ACCV, IJCNLP-AACL, CVPR-W, etc.
