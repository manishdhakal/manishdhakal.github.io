<b>CV/NLP</b>

Manish Dhakal is a Ph.D. student in Industrial Engineering at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, supervised by Dr. Yi Ding. He completed his M.S. in Computer Science at Georgia State University. His primary line of research is within <b>model compression</b> and <b>efficient learning</b> of computer vision (CV) and natural language processing (NLP) applications. His publications have been presented at premier venues, such as WACV, MICCAI, ACCV, IJCNLP-AACL, CVPR-W, etc.
