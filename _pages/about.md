<b>Efficient ML | CV | NLP</b>

Manish Dhakal is a Ph.D. student in Industrial Engineering at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, supervised by Dr. Yi Ding. He received his M.S. in Computer Science from Georgia State University. His research focuses on <b>efficient adaptation</b> and <b>knowledge compression</b> of foundation models, from large language models (LLMs) and vision-language models to vision-language-action (VLA) models, making them cheaper to specialize and deployable on resource-constrained hardware. His publications have been presented at premier venues, such as EMNLP, WACV, MICCAI, ACCV, IJCNLP-AACL, CVPR-W, etc.
